Angry Archers
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

In short: we don't ask for an account, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location. Online play uses a small relay server to connect you with your opponent. The game has no ads and no analytics.

Angry Archers ("the game") is developed by Manimode Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or precise location.

Online play

When you play online with a friend, the game connects to our relay server so the two of you can be paired using a 4-letter room code. To do this, the server necessarily receives:

  • your device's IP address (a normal part of any internet connection),
  • the room code, and
  • the gameplay messages (such as aim and shot data) that it forwards to the other player.

This information is used only to connect you with your opponent. It is not stored after the session ends. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to identify you. Our server is hosted by Render, which may keep standard technical logs under its own privacy policy.

Offline modes (vs BOT, and two players on one device) do not send any data.

Permissions

The game requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet - to enable online play.
  • Vibrate - to give feedback when an archer is hit.

The game does not access your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Third-party services

The game currently does not include advertising, analytics, or other third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children's privacy

Angry Archers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

Connections to our relay server are made over the internet, and we keep the data it handles to the minimum needed to run a match. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we do not retain personal information that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data (for example, by adding ads or analytics), we will update this policy and the "last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to make a request about your data, email us at manimode.studio@gmail.com.
