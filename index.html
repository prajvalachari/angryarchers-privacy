Angry Archers
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short: we don't ask for an account, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location. Online play uses a small relay server to connect you with your opponent. The game has no analytics of its own, and it needs an internet connection to run.

Angry Archers ("the game") is developed by Manimode Studio ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or precise location.

You can type a nickname (up to 12 characters) in the game. It is saved only on your device, together with your settings (theme, sound, music and blood on or off). In online play, your nickname is sent to your opponent so they can see it during the match.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. We show banner ads, full-screen ads (for example between matches or when you change the theme), and ads when you return to the game after leaving it for a while.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (such as your city or region),
  • how you interact with ads and the app, and
  • crash logs and diagnostics.

We do not receive this data and do not sell it. Google's use of it is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • On Android you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization under Settings, then Google, then Ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent form from Google the first time you play, and asks for your choice before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings, then Privacy settings, inside the game.

Online play

When you play online with a friend, the game connects to our relay server so the two of you can be paired using a 4-letter room code. To do this, the server necessarily receives:

  • your device's IP address (a normal part of any internet connection),
  • the room code,
  • your nickname, and
  • the gameplay messages (such as aim and shot data) that it forwards to the other player.

This information is used only to connect you with your opponent. It is not stored after the session ends. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to identify you. Our server is hosted by Render, which may keep standard technical logs under its own privacy policy.

Offline modes (vs BOT, and two players on one device) do not send gameplay data to our server.

Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to run. When it starts, it makes a small request to a Google server to check that you are online. No personal information is sent in that request.

Permissions

The game requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet - for online play, ads, and the connection check.
  • Network state - used by Google's ad software to check your connection.
  • Advertising ID - used by Google's ad software to show and measure ads.
  • Vibrate - to give feedback when an archer is hit.

The game does not access your storage, camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (advertising and consent messages) - see the Advertising section above.
  • Render - hosts our relay server for online play.

The game does not include other advertising or analytics services.

Children's privacy

Angry Archers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

Connections to our relay server and to Google's services are made over the internet, and we keep the data our server handles to the minimum needed to run a match. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we do not retain personal information that could be exposed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this policy and the "last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want to make a request about your data, email us at manimode.studio@gmail.com.
